What Is It · Reading 2 of 5
Why the walls are not equally thick

Each chamber’s wall is as thick as its job needs. The atria push blood only a short way, so their walls are thin. The right ventricle sends blood to the lungs, which are close by.

ventricle A lower chamber of the heart. It pumps blood out of the heart.

The left ventricle sends blood to the whole body. It has the thickest muscle and makes the highest pressure.
